# Minikube Local Host Resolution
#
# To route traffic to your tenant Ingress resources, map your Minikube IP
# to the tenant domain names in your hosts file:
#
# Windows: C:\Windows\System32\drivers\etc\hosts
# Linux/macOS: /etc/hosts
#
# Format:
# <MINIKUBE_IP> alpha.nuclear.local beta.nuclear.local
#
# Example (replace 192.168.49.2 with `minikube ip`):
# 192.168.49.2 alpha.nuclear.local beta.nuclear.local
